The club

Built on formation, character and community

We are a grassroots to adult football club in Southern California dedicated to long-term player formation. We discover talent, build character, give exposure and create a strong community through the beautiful game.

2025Established
100%Dedication
SoCalBased

Our owner is dedicated to the growth of football in Southern California. With years of experience as a player and coach along with a passion for the grassroots level, TSCFG is driven for the community and by the with a vision of Worldwide success.

“Football is more than just a game; it's a tool for character building and community strength. At TSCFG, we strive to provide the best environment for our players to thrive both on and off the pitch.”— The Owner, TSCFG
